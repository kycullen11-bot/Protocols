Strength —

Six sessions per 28 days. Full body, minimum effective volume, 45–65 min. The sequence is A → B → C whatever the calendar does — a missed session is skipped, never repaid.

Progression —
Don't add load when: sleep averaged under 7 h over the previous 3–4 days · you're Yellow or Red · you only finished by grinding · dizziness is clearly worse for hours after · resting HR and fatigue are both trending wrong · performance dropped across two consecutive sessions.
Zone 2 —
Vestibular / PPPD

The highest-value item in the whole protocol, and it happens every day including night shifts. Avoidance is what keeps PPPD running — mild provocation is the treatment, not a side effect.

The ladder ten rungs
Two timing rules. Never do provocative work within 2 hours of driving to patrol, and never within 2 hours of bed. On strength days, put the session after lifting or several hours away from it.
