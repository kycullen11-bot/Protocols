The 2 A.M. Rule

Answer these before you act. If you can't answer confidently — pause, gather more, call a supervisor, get a warrant.

1
What crime, if any, am I investigating?
2
What level of suspicion do I have right now — none, reasonable suspicion, or probable cause?
3
Am I detaining or arresting?
4
Am I searching? What specific authority allows it?
5
Am I entering a home? Warrant or a valid exception?
6
Could I explain this clearly to a judge six months from now?
